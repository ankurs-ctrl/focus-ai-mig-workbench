## 0. Goal and constraints

**Goal:** a 37-target Auto-map on UAT finishes in about 2 minutes and returns real mappings for most targets. Any incomplete coverage must be visible to the user.

**Constraints**
- Keep the whole-table rule: never split one table's semantic bundle across scan batches.
- Keep all original backend context. "Dropping" context means leaving it out of later model messages, not deleting it.
- Never invent mappings, and never call partial coverage an exhaustive no-match.
- Keep completed mappings when another batch fails.
- Do not raise limits or add workers as a substitute for fixing the causes below.
- Do not reset or release permits, change grants or shared agents, or resize the compute pool without explicit approval.
- Publish through PRs and keep the protected deployers and UAT `client.env`.
- Treat green containers or CI as health only. Acceptance means a real Auto-map run on UAT.

## 3. Findings

### A. Stale compiled workspace data can overflow every scan message (verified)

Job `f1875a49-32fa-548b-8d0f-4280a4cdb064` failed in 15 seconds with `AUTO_MAP_PREPARATION_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`. A single target, `SOURCE_OF_BUSINESS`, needed 160,192 characters against 120,000.

I compared its saved request with the passing job `4ebf6266-db6f-5216-9f60-dfc3524edfdb`. Every request field is the same size except the workspace:

| Workspace field | Added characters |
|---|---|
| `compiled_mapping_context_hash` (holds serialized compiler input starting `{"compilerContractVersion"…`, not a hash) | +74,247 |
| `auto_map_outcome` (proposals from a previous run) | +8,537 |
| `compiled_mapping_sql` | +6,908 |

Workspace total went from 168,053 to 267,555. After the scan code's exclusions, what is left grew from about 22,000 to about 118,000 characters. Without those four fields it is about 22,000 again.

- **Code:** `automap_scan_context.py:373-377` keeps these fields in the shared message. `assistant_retrieval.py:54-65` already moves the same fields out for chat.
- **Fresh mapping check:** mapping 2701 (fresh upload) has those fields empty, and preparation passed.
- **Consequence:** whether Auto-map works depends on UI state, such as compiling first or having run Auto-map before.

### B. Permit contention kills scan shards before they reach the model (verified)

Across the five batches of job `dae0aa56-6523-5400-b580-3f624350c254`, about 20 scan calls were attempted:
- 3 succeeded.
- About 16 failed with `AdmissionUnavailable` without any model call.
- 2 returned `InvalidScanResponse`.

I read these outcomes from the archive rows (`value.row.status` and `error_code`).

- **Mechanism (`automap_admission.py:237-306, 506-512`):** every waiting scan thread retries `try_claim` every 0.05 seconds. Each retry opens a new session and runs a transaction (inventory SELECT, then UPDATE) on the 4-row permit table. `LOCK_TIMEOUT` is 1 second and the statement timeout is 5 seconds. Any error is raised as `AdmissionUnavailable` (line 275).
- **Terminal failure (`automap_scan.py:445-457`):** the scan worker records any such error as `ADMISSION_FAILED` for that shard and never retries.
- **Contention:** up to 5 batches × 4 scan threads means 20 pollers on 4 slots.
- **Evidence from the permit table's statement history (10:05:30-10:12):**
  - 82 claim `UPDATE`s succeeded, averaging 2.0 seconds, up to 5.6 seconds, with transaction blocking up to 4.4 seconds.
  - 3 `UPDATE`s failed with timeout code 630.
  - 137 inventory `SELECT`s, averaging 168 milliseconds.
- **Inference:** the lock timeouts and statement timeouts are what raise `AdmissionUnavailable`. I did not capture the exception text, because it is discarded.

### C. Scan calls are too slow and too large (verified sizes and gaps, estimated latency)

- Scan messages were 117,708 to 118,508 characters, against a 120,000 limit.
- The largest gaps between archive writes were 110, 50, 57, 93 and 82 seconds. I take those to be single scan calls of 50 to 110 seconds.
- The scan agent's own budget is 120 seconds.
- Most of each message is shared context repeated in every scan. The bigger batches (5, 5, 9, 9, 9 targets) also leave 3 whole tables unscanned (670 records) per batch, against 1 table (603 records) with the earlier 2-target batches.
- **Arithmetic:** roughly 27 scan calls through 4 permits at about 60 seconds is about 7 rounds, roughly 7 minutes. Two minutes is not reachable at this message size.
- The same source tables are scanned once per batch, so five times over.

## 4. What to fix, in priority order

1. **Admission:**
   - Queue permit requests in the worker with a single in-process wait, and make one database claim per call instead of polling every 50 milliseconds.
   - Retry transient lock and timeout errors with backoff, and never treat them as a terminal shard failure.
   - Record the real exception type for any failure. Keep `ADMISSION_FAILED` only for real refusals, and show it in the coverage warnings.
2. **Message size:**
   - Remove `compiled_mapping_*`, `auto_map_outcome`, `pending_mapping_proposals`, `conversation_history` and `validation_history` from the scan message. Keep them in the saved request.
   - Include shared target and table semantics once per message instead of repeating them.
   - Account for every omitted field in the field coverage record.
   - Check the same size calculation in the pre-check and the runtime.
3. **Fewer, lighter calls:**
   - Stop scanning the same tables once per batch. Either scan once for all targets or share scan results across batches.
   - Choose the batch count by weighing concurrency against how many tables fall out of scope.
   - Reduce shards per batch.
4. **Time budget:**
   - Count permit waiting and retrieval separately from the per-call limit.
   - Raise `AUTOMAP_CONTEXT_TARGET_SECONDS` only if measurement shows it is needed (maximum 600).
   - Stop using `from None` for archive errors. Log the real cause without logging evidence.
5. **Agent response handling:**
   - Log the byte size and shape of a non-JSON response, without its content.
   - Add a single bounded retry for non-JSON responses.
   - Log the `ValueError` text from validation.
6. **Frontend:** `compiledMappingContextHash` should hold a short digest, not 74,000 characters of compiler input.
7. **Stuck job:** the stuck-job question needs a decision first. Mark orphaned jobs failed or resumable once the lease has expired, and keep their saved batches.
8. **Permit capacity:** raising it from 4 needs reprovisioning and approval. Do not change it as part of the code fix.
9. **Timing logs:** per-stage timings for dispatch, retrieval, permit wait, each scan call, reduction, archive write, agent call and validation.
